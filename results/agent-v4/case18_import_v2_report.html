ledgerly/importers.py:112 · correctness

However, add_expense() (ledgerly/expenses.py:24) inserts via db.execute(), and Database.execute() (ledgerly/db.py:126-129) itself opens its own `with self.transaction():` and calls self.conn.commit() right after the INSERT.
